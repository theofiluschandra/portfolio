01 — Our Story

More than a meal.

There was a time when every neighbourhood had a kopitiam.

A place where uncles read the morning newspaper, families shared one table, friends argued over football and everyone knew exactly how they liked their kopi.

KOPI 88 brings that feeling back — with recipes inspired by Chinese-Malayan heritage and a space made for today's generation.
