OUR STORY

Rooted in ritual.

SIGMATCHA started with a simple idea: create a place where people could slow down, enjoy beautifully crafted matcha and feel at home.

Inspired by Japanese tea culture and modern café living, we carefully source our matcha and pair it with familiar flavours from around the world.
