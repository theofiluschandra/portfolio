About Langgeng Jaya Group

Industrial expertise with a circular mindset.

Send your product, target specification, estimated quantity, destination, and preferred timeline using the enquiry form. Our team can follow up to clarify requirements.

Sample availability depends on the product and specification. Mention your sample request in the enquiry form so the team can advise on the next step.

MOQ varies by product, grade, and customization. Contact the team with your expected quantity to discuss the applicable requirements.

Shipping arrangements depend on volume, destination, and buyer requirements. Include the delivery destination in your enquiry.
